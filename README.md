
# Suraksha Shield

Suraksha Shield is a lightweight browser-based risk-detection prototype designed to help citizens identify and understand emerging digital public-safety threats.

---

## Overview

The rapid growth of digital services in India has created new opportunities for citizens, businesses, and communities — but it has also introduced new forms of digital risk.

Many users may encounter suspicious digital content without knowing:

- Whether something is potentially dangerous
- Why it may be risky
- What warning signs they should look for
- What action they should take next

**Suraksha Shield** addresses this gap through a browser-based risk-detection experience that aims to make digital safety more understandable and accessible.

---

## Problem Statement

Digital threats are becoming increasingly sophisticated, while public awareness often remains limited.

### The Challenge

> **How can we make digital risk detection understandable and accessible to ordinary citizens?**

Suraksha Shield explores this problem through a lightweight browser-based prototype.

---

## Our Solution

Suraksha Shield provides a simple interface where digital risks can be evaluated and communicated to users in an understandable manner.

The core philosophy is:

### Detect
Identify potential indicators of digital risk.

### Protect
Help users make more informed decisions before interacting with potentially harmful content.

### Educate
Improve awareness by helping users understand common warning signs and safe digital practices.

## Threat Categories

Suraksha Shield focuses on three major digital public-safety threat categories.

### 1. Phishing and Online Scams

Phishing involves fraudulent messages, websites, emails, or links designed to trick users into sharing sensitive information.

Citizens may encounter these threats through:

- Fake banking and UPI messages
- Fraudulent payment links
- Fake customer support websites
- Suspicious emails and SMS messages
- Impersonation of government organizations or trusted brands

**Potential Warning Signs:**
- Unfamiliar or misspelled website URLs
- Requests for OTPs, PINs, or banking credentials
- Urgent messages demanding immediate action
- Unexpected payment or reward notifications

### 2. Malicious and Suspicious Digital Content

Malicious digital content may expose users to harmful websites, downloads, or online resources.

Citizens may encounter these threats through:

- Suspicious website links
- Unknown file downloads
- Fake applications
- Untrusted third-party websites
- Misleading advertisements and redirects

**Potential Warning Signs:**
- Unexpected file downloads
- Unusual website permissions
- Unknown or untrusted sources
- Requests to install unfamiliar applications
- Suspicious redirects and pop-ups

### 3. Online Fraud and Digital Impersonation

Online fraud and digital impersonation involve individuals or organizations pretending to be legitimate entities to deceive users.

Citizens may encounter these threats through:

- Fake social media profiles
- Impersonation of government officials
- Fraudulent job and internship offers
- Fake investment opportunities
- Lottery and prize scams
- Fraudulent customer support representatives

**Potential Warning Signs:**
- Unrealistic financial promises
- Requests for advance payments
- Unverified identities
- Pressure to share personal information
- Inconsistent contact details

## How It Works

The prototype follows a simple risk-assessment workflow:

1. **User Input:** The user provides digital content or information for evaluation.
2. **Risk Analysis:** The system examines available indicators associated with potential threats.
3. **Risk Assessment:** Potential warning signs are evaluated.
4. **Risk Classification:** The system communicates the identified risk level.
5. **User Awareness:** The user receives understandable information to support safer digital decisions.
