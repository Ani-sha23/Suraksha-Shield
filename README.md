# Suraksha Shield

Suraksha Shield is a lightweight browser-based risk-detection prototype designed to help citizens identify and understand emerging digital public-safety threats.



---

## Overview

The rapid growth of digital services in India has created new opportunities for citizens, businesses, and communities — but it has also introduced new forms of digital risk.

Many users may encounter suspicious digital content without knowing:

- Whether something is potentially dangerous
- Why it may be risky
- What warning signs they should look for and what action they should take next

**Suraksha Shield** addresses this gap through a browser-based risk-detection experience.

---

## Problem Statement

Digital threats are becoming increasingly sophisticated, while public awareness often remains limited.

Traditional cybersecurity solutions can be:

- Difficult for non-technical users
- Complex to understand
- Focused primarily on technical users or organizations
- Less effective at explaining *why* something is suspicious


### The challenge

> **How can we make digital risk detection understandable and accessible to ordinary citizens?**

Suraksha Shield explores this problem through a lightweight browser-based prototype.

---

## Our Solution

Suraksha Shield provides a simple interface where digital risks can be evaluated and communicated to the user in an understandable manner.

The core philosophy is:

### Detect
Identify potential indicators of digital risk.

### Explain
Clearly communicate why something may be suspicious or unsafe.

### Protect
Help users make more informed decisions before interacting with potentially harmful content.

### Educate
Improve awareness by helping users understand common warning signs.

---

## Key Features

- **Browser-Based Protection**
  - Lightweight and accessible through a web interface.

- **Risk Detection**
  - Designed to identify indicators associated with digital public-safety threats.

- **Risk Awareness**
  - Presents potential risks in a user-friendly format.

- **Simple Risk Interpretation**
  - Helps users understand the severity or nature of a detected risk.

- **Citizen-Centric Design**
  - Built with non-technical users in mind.

- **Lightweight Prototype**
  - Designed to provide a simple and accessible experience without unnecessary complexity.

- **India-Focused**
  - The project is designed around emerging digital public-safety challenges faced by citizens in India.
